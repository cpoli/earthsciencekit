Atmosphere: units, equations and validation#

Domain hub · History · Examples · API reference

Conventions and model limits#

Pressure is Pa, temperature K, height m and latitude radians. The isothermal barometric model assumes constant temperature; the dry-ascent model has a changing temperature and should not be compared with that profile directly. Potential temperature uses a caller-supplied reference pressure.

Mixing ratio is water mass per dry-air mass; specific humidity is water mass per total moist-air mass. Relative humidity is a fraction and can exceed one. Saturation vapor pressure is a constant-latent-heat Clausius-Clapeyron approximation over liquid water, restricted to 180-330 K. It is not saturation over ice. Dry parcel ascent excludes condensation.

The two-layer temperature profile has a constant lapse rate up to the tropopause and is isothermal above. Richardson number and the surface pressure tendency are signed. Rossby-wave speed takes the total horizontal wavenumber in rad/m and returns the eastward phase speed. The barotropic vorticity model uses a doubly periodic beta plane, so its zonal and meridional boundaries are artificial.

N squared is signed: negative values indicate instability. Geostrophic speed and Rossby scaling reject exactly zero Coriolis parameter, but the caller must also recognize their poor applicability near the equator.

Input contracts#

Algebraic models accept finite real scalars or nonempty arrays. Booleans, complex inputs, nonnumeric values and NaN/Inf are rejected. Physical bounds apply element by element; NumPy broadcasting applies between arguments. No unit metadata are carried in raw numbers. Bounds protect mathematical domains and obvious physical contradictions, but do not establish model applicability.

Equation and output map#

dry_air_density#

earthsciencekit.atmosphere.dry_air_density()

Output: kg/m^3. Dry ideal air with fixed specific gas constant.

pressure / (R_DRY_AIR * temperature)

scale_height#

earthsciencekit.atmosphere.scale_height()

Output: m. Dry isothermal atmosphere with constant gravity.

R_DRY_AIR * temperature / gravity

barometric_pressure#

earthsciencekit.atmosphere.barometric_pressure()

Output: Pa. Isothermal column; signed height is relative to the reference level.

surface_pressure * np.exp(-gravity * height / (R_DRY_AIR * temperature))

potential_temperature#

earthsciencekit.atmosphere.potential_temperature()

Output: K. Dry ideal gas with constant heat capacity; not equivalent potential temperature.

temperature * (reference_pressure / pressure)**(R_DRY_AIR / CP_DRY_AIR)

temperature_from_potential#

earthsciencekit.atmosphere.temperature_from_potential()

Output: K. Uses the same dry-air heat capacity and reference pressure convention.

potential_temperature * (pressure / reference_pressure)**(R_DRY_AIR / CP_DRY_AIR)

dry_adiabatic_lapse_rate#

earthsciencekit.atmosphere.dry_adiabatic_lapse_rate()

Output: K/m. Returned positive lapse magnitude in K per metre; temperature decreases upward.

gravity / heat_capacity

saturation_vapor_pressure#

earthsciencekit.atmosphere.saturation_vapor_pressure()

Output: Pa. Liquid-water reference with constant latent heat; restricted to 180-330 K, not an ice saturation formula.

611.2 * np.exp((2.5e6 / R_VAPOR) * (1 / 273.15 - 1 / temperature))

mixing_ratio#

earthsciencekit.atmosphere.mixing_ratio()

Output: kg/kg dry air. Ideal vapor mixture; vapor pressure must be below total pressure.

EPSILON_WATER * vapor_pressure / (total_pressure - vapor_pressure)

specific_humidity#

earthsciencekit.atmosphere.specific_humidity()

Output: kg/kg moist air. Output is water mass per total moist-air mass.

mixing_ratio / (1 + mixing_ratio)

virtual_temperature#

earthsciencekit.atmosphere.virtual_temperature()

Output: K. Ideal vapor mixture without suspended liquid or ice.

temperature * (1 + (1 / EPSILON_WATER - 1) * specific_humidity)

relative_humidity#

earthsciencekit.atmosphere.relative_humidity()

Output: 1. Returned fraction may exceed one during supersaturation; no clipping.

vapor_pressure / saturation_pressure

coriolis_parameter#

earthsciencekit.atmosphere.coriolis_parameter()

Output: 1/s. Spherical rotating-Earth approximation; input is radians.

2 * OMEGA_EARTH * np.sin(latitude)

brunt_vaisala_squared#

earthsciencekit.atmosphere.brunt_vaisala_squared()

Output: 1/s^2. Returns signed N squared; negative values represent instability rather than an imaginary frequency.

gravity * vertical_gradient / potential_temperature

geostrophic_speed#

earthsciencekit.atmosphere.geostrophic_speed()

Output: m/s. Signed cross-gradient component; invalid at the equator where coriolis vanishes.

pressure_gradient / (density * coriolis)

rossby_number#

earthsciencekit.atmosphere.rossby_number()

Output: 1. Magnitude scaling only; small values support but do not prove geostrophic balance.

speed / (np.abs(coriolis) * length)

barometer_column_height#

earthsciencekit.atmosphere.barometer_column_height()

Output: m. Incompressible liquid with a vacuum above the column; vapour pressure and capillarity are neglected.

pressure / (liquid_density * gravity)

two_layer_temperature#

earthsciencekit.atmosphere.two_layer_temperature()

Output: K. Constant-lapse troposphere under an isothermal lower stratosphere; the stratospheric warming above about 20 km is omitted.

surface_temperature - lapse_rate * np.minimum(height, tropopause_height)

richardson_number#

earthsciencekit.atmosphere.richardson_number()

Output: 1. Signed; stratified shear flow can become unstable only where it falls below 1/4 (Miles-Howard).

brunt_vaisala_squared / shear**2

surface_pressure_tendency#

earthsciencekit.atmosphere.surface_pressure_tendency()

Output: Pa/s. Hydrostatic column with no surface mass flux; divergence at one level is far larger than the column mean.

-surface_pressure * mean_divergence

rossby_wave_speed#

earthsciencekit.atmosphere.rossby_wave_speed()

Output: m/s. Nondivergent barotropic flow on a beta plane; positive values are eastward.

zonal_wind - beta / wavenumber**2

simulate_barotropic_vorticity#

earthsciencekit.atmosphere.simulate_barotropic_vorticity()

Output: 1/s, relative vorticity on a doubly periodic grid, flattened row by row. Pseudo-spectral derivatives with two-thirds dealiasing; the domain mean is removed. No forcing, friction, divergence or sphere.

d(zeta)/dt = -J(psi, zeta) - beta * d(psi)/dx,  laplacian(psi) = zeta

Independent validation#

Every algebraic API has a fixed-value check, broadcasting check and invalid-input contracts. The shared suite adds cross-model identities, energy/conservation checks and independent analytic solutions for dynamics. See Validation policy for the exact evidence and Numerical methods and sampled results for numerical conventions.

Volcanology: units, equations and validation#

Domain hub · History · Examples · API reference

Conventions and model limits#

Ages, half-lives and travel times are seconds and decay constants 1/s; convert with units.convert(value, "Ma", "s") (kyr, Ma and Ga are available). The uranium decay constants LAMBDA_U238 and LAMBDA_U235 (Jaffey et al. 1971), the present U238_U235 = 137.88 and Libby’s mean life LIBBY_MEAN_LIFE (5568-year half-life) live in earthsciencekit.constants. Every dating model assumes a closed system: no parent or daughter gained or lost after time zero, except where the concordia chord models episodic lead loss. radiometric_age and radiogenic_daughter assume no initial daughter; isochron_fit removes that assumption with an unweighted least-squares line and requires a positive slope. radiocarbon_age is a conventional radiocarbon age, not a calibrated calendar age. lead_lead_age solves for ages between one year and 10 Ga. concordia_ratios returns [207Pb*/235U, 206Pb*/238U] on the last axis, the horizontal and vertical coordinates of Wetherill’s diagram.

Isotope ratios are heavy over light; delta values are per mil relative to a caller-supplied standard. Fractionation factors are product over reservoir. isotope_temperature is the Epstein et al. (1953) calibration, both delta values on the same scale, valid near 7-30 degC. Crystallization concentrations carry the unit of the initial concentration, and partition coefficients are bulk solid over melt. lattice_strain_partition takes radii in metres and the apparent Young’s modulus of the site in pascals.

Viscosities are Pa s for a crystal- and bubble-free Newtonian melt. Shaw’s slope s is computed from the molar composition (Shaw 1972, Table 1) and is supplied by the caller; his law is Arrhenius with activation energy s * 1e4 * R_GAS. Water solubility is a mass fraction with a square-root pressure law and no CO2. Dike flow is laminar plane Poiseuille flow with a positive density contrast meaning buoyant magma. The Mogi source returns [radial, vertical] displacements on the last axis, outward and upward positive, for a source small compared with its depth.

Plume buoyancy flux is reduced_gravity * velocity * radius**2 without the factor pi, as in Morton, Taylor and Turner (1956) and Briggs (1969). column_height takes the heat flux in watts. The Volcanic Explosivity Index uses the bulk tephra volume only.

Input contracts#

Algebraic models accept finite real scalars or nonempty arrays. Booleans, complex inputs, nonnumeric values and NaN/Inf are rejected. Physical bounds apply element by element; NumPy broadcasting applies between arguments, except for the vector inputs of isochron_fit. No unit metadata are carried in raw numbers. Bounds protect mathematical domains and obvious physical contradictions, but do not establish model applicability.

Equation and output map#

rayleigh_fractionation#

earthsciencekit.volcanology.rayleigh_fractionation()

Output: 1. Rayleigh (1896): the product leaves as soon as it forms, the reservoir stays well mixed and the factor is constant.

initial_ratio * remaining_fraction**(fractionation_factor - 1)

delta_value#

earthsciencekit.volcanology.delta_value()

Output: per mil. Linear in the ratio; delta values of different standards must be converted before they are compared.

(ratio / standard_ratio - 1) * 1000

decay_constant#

earthsciencekit.volcanology.decay_constant()

Output: 1/s. Rutherford and Soddy (1902): first-order decay, independent of temperature, pressure and chemistry.

np.log(2) / half_life

parent_fraction#

earthsciencekit.volcanology.parent_fraction()

Output: 1. Rutherford and Soddy (1902): a closed system with no parent added after time zero.

np.exp(-decay_constant * time)

radiometric_age#

earthsciencekit.volcanology.radiometric_age()

Output: s. Closed system with no initial daughter; Boltwood (1907) applied it to lead in uranium minerals.

np.log1p(daughter_parent_ratio) / decay_constant

radiogenic_daughter#

earthsciencekit.volcanology.radiogenic_daughter()

Output: unit of parent. Closed system with no initial daughter; the inverse of radiometric_age. Holmes (1913) used it to build the first radiometric time scale.

parent * np.expm1(decay_constant * time)

fractional_crystallization_melt#

earthsciencekit.volcanology.fractional_crystallization_melt()

Output: unit of initial_concentration. Rayleigh fractional crystallization (Bowen 1928; Gast 1968): crystals do not re-equilibrate and the bulk partition coefficient is constant.

initial_concentration * melt_fraction**(partition_coefficient - 1)

equilibrium_crystallization_melt#

earthsciencekit.volcanology.equilibrium_crystallization_melt()

Output: unit of initial_concentration. Batch mass balance (Shaw 1970): solid and melt stay in equilibrium, so an incompatible element is enriched at most by 1 / partition_coefficient.

initial_concentration / (partition_coefficient + melt_fraction * (1 - partition_coefficient))

lattice_strain_partition#

earthsciencekit.volcanology.lattice_strain_partition()

Output: 1. Blundy and Wood (1994) quantification of Goldschmidt (1937) for isovalent ions on one site; charge differences need a separate parabola.

peak_partition * np.exp(-4 * np.pi * youngs_modulus * AVOGADRO * (optimal_radius / 2 * (ionic_radius - optimal_radius)**2 + (ionic_radius - optimal_radius)**3 / 3) / (R_GAS * temperature))

isotope_temperature#

earthsciencekit.volcanology.isotope_temperature()

Output: K. Urey (1947) predicted the effect; this is the empirical calibration of Epstein, Buchsbaum, Lowenstam and Urey (1953), valid for about 7-30 degC and equilibrium calcite.

273.15 + 16.5 - 4.3 * d + 0.14 * d**2

radiocarbon_age#

earthsciencekit.volcanology.radiocarbon_age()

Output: s. Libby et al. (1949) with the conventional 5568-year (Libby) half-life, mean life LIBBY_MEAN_LIFE; it is a radiocarbon age, not a calendar age, until calibrated.

-LIBBY_MEAN_LIFE * np.log(fraction_modern)

radiogenic_lead_ratio#

earthsciencekit.volcanology.radiogenic_lead_ratio()

Output: 1. Patterson (1956): closed system with today’s 238U/235U = U238_U235 and the Jaffey et al. (1971) decay constants. The ratio tends to LAMBDA_U235 / (U238_U235 * LAMBDA_U238) at zero age.

np.expm1(LAMBDA_U235 * time) / (U238_U235 * np.expm1(LAMBDA_U238 * time))

lead_lead_age#

earthsciencekit.volcanology.lead_lead_age()

Output: s. Inverts radiogenic_lead_ratio by Brent’s method between one year and 10 Ga; same closed-system assumptions.

radiogenic_lead_ratio(age) = lead_ratio

concordia_ratios#

earthsciencekit.volcanology.concordia_ratios()

Output: [1, 1]. Wetherill (1956): closed system with the Jaffey et al. (1971) decay constants. Episodic lead loss puts samples on a straight chord (discordia) between the concordia points of formation and loss.

[np.expm1(LAMBDA_U235 * time), np.expm1(LAMBDA_U238 * time)]

plume_rise_height#

earthsciencekit.volcanology.plume_rise_height()

Output: m. Morton, Taylor and Turner (1956) for a steady point source of buoyancy in uniform stratification, with Briggs’s constant 5.0 (entrainment about 0.1); source momentum, wind and moisture are neglected.

5.0 * buoyancy_flux**0.25 * buoyancy_frequency**-0.75

mogi_displacement#

earthsciencekit.volcanology.mogi_displacement()

Output: [m, m]. Mogi (1958) point source in an elastic half-space; the chamber radius must be small compared with its depth. The uplift halves at radial_distance = 0.766 * depth.

(1 - poisson_ratio) * volume_change / np.pi * [radial_distance, depth] / (radial_distance**2 + depth**2)**1.5

isochron_fit#

earthsciencekit.volcanology.isochron_fit()

Output: [s, 1]. Nicolaysen (1961): every sample formed at the same time with the same initial ratio and stayed closed; ordinary least squares without measurement-error weighting (a York fit would weight them). The slope must be positive.

daughter_ratio = initial_ratio + parent_ratio * np.expm1(decay_constant * age)

arrhenius_viscosity#

earthsciencekit.volcanology.arrhenius_viscosity()

Output: Pa s. Constant activation energy over the range of interest; crystal- and bubble-free Newtonian melt.

reference_viscosity * np.exp(activation_energy / R_GAS * (1 / temperature - 1 / reference_temperature))

shaw_viscosity#

earthsciencekit.volcanology.shaw_viscosity()

Output: Pa s. Shaw (1972), Am. J. Sci. 272: 870-893; all lines pass near ln(eta [poise]) = -6.4 at 1e4/T = 1.5. Crystal- and bubble-free Newtonian melt; about 0.2 log units of accuracy.

0.1 * np.exp(slope * (1e4 / temperature) - 1.5 * slope - 6.4)

column_height#

earthsciencekit.volcanology.column_height()

Output: m. Wilson, Sparks, Huang and Watkins (1978), JGR 83: 1829-1836, for a standard atmosphere and a strong steady column; wind bends weak plumes lower.

8.2 * heat_flux**0.25

dike_ascent_velocity#

earthsciencekit.volcanology.dike_ascent_velocity()

Output: m/s. Wilson and Head (1981): plane Poiseuille flow between parallel walls driven by buoyancy alone; check that the Reynolds number magma_density * velocity * width / viscosity stays below about 2000.

width**2 * density_contrast * gravity / (12 * viscosity)

water_solubility#

earthsciencekit.volcanology.water_solubility()

Output: 1 (mass fraction). Square-root (Henry-type) law for pure water vapour, valid up to a few hundred MPa; ignores CO2 and temperature.

solubility_constant * np.sqrt(pressure)

volcanic_explosivity_index#

earthsciencekit.volcanology.volcanic_explosivity_index()

Output: 1. Newhall and Self (1982), volume criterion only: VEI 0 below 1e4 m^3, 1 below 1e6 m^3, then one step per factor of ten up to VEI 8 at 1e12 m^3 and above. Column height and other criteria are not used.

np.where(erupted_volume < 1e4, 0, np.where(erupted_volume < 1e6, 1, np.clip(np.floor(np.log10(erupted_volume)) - 4, 2, 8)))

Eruption column#

earthsciencekit.volcanology.simulate_eruption_column() integrates the top-hat plume fluxes Q = b**2 w, M = b**2 w**2 and F = b**2 w g' along the travel time of a parcel on the axis: dz/dt = M / Q, dQ/dt = 2 alpha abs(M)**1.5 / Q, dM/dt = F and dF/dt = -N**2 M. It returns the parcel height, vertical velocity and reduced gravity. With N = 0 it follows the pure-plume similarity solution; in a uniform atmosphere its top lies near plume_rise_height for the default entrainment 0.12. Past the top it continues as a fountain that falls back and oscillates about the neutral buoyancy level, an illustrative closure.

Independent validation#

Every algebraic API has a fixed-value check, broadcasting or bound checks and invalid-input contracts. The volcanology tests also check that parent and daughter atoms are conserved and that the age equation inverts accumulation, that Libby’s mean life gives a 5568-year half-life, that the lead ratio tends to its zero-age limit and lead_lead_age inverts it, that both concordia clocks return the same age and lead loss falls on a straight chord, that the isochron recovers its age and initial ratio, that fractional crystallization conserves mass and shares its initial slope with equilibrium crystallization, that lattice strain peaks at the optimal radius, that Shaw’s lines share their pivot, that the Mogi uplift integrates to 2 (1 - nu) dV, and that the eruption column follows the pure-plume similarity solution and reaches the Morton-Taylor-Turner height. See Validation policy for the exact evidence and Numerical methods and sampled results for numerical conventions.

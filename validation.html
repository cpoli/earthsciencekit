Validation policy#

Validation has three separate goals: reject invalid inputs, verify numerical implementation and explain physical applicability. None substitutes for another.

Tests include fixed reference values for all 237 algebraic models, elementwise physical bounds, finite/real contracts and broadcasting. Cross-model tests cover Euler-vector tangency and circuit closure, the load balance of a flexed plate, ocean subsidence against integrated heat loss, wave-interface energy conservation, magnitude inversion, moisture-ratio inversion and shallow/deep wave limits. Climate tests check the top-of-atmosphere balance of a greenhouse layer, the global mean of daily insolation, closure of every ice-albedo equilibrium, the variance of the red-noise spectrum and recovery of one-box parameters by the Gregory regression. Oceanography tests check that the integrated Ekman spiral equals the Ekman transport, that the Stommel gyre interior matches Sverdrup transport, that the abyssal profile solves its advection-diffusion equation, and that the simplified seawater density reduces to the linear law near its reference and cabbels. Geophysics tests check that the Bouguer anomaly recovers a buried body under topography, that Clairaut’s formula matches Somigliana’s normal gravity, that the numerical Stokes integral of a zonal anomaly gives the 1 / (n - 1) law, that the degree-1 zonal field is the axial dipole, that virtual geomagnetic poles of a tilted dipole return its pole and that an infinite magnetized layer has no external field. Volcanology tests check that decay conserves parent plus daughter atoms and the age equation inverts accumulation, that the Pb-Pb age inverts the radiogenic lead ratio, that both concordia clocks agree and lead loss falls on a chord, that the isochron recovers its age, that fractional crystallization conserves mass, that lattice strain peaks at the optimal radius, that Shaw’s lines share their pivot and that the Mogi uplift integrates to 2 (1 - nu) dV. Hydrology tests check that every section of Dupuit’s water table carries the Dupuit discharge, that steady Richards profiles carry a constant flux, that the Green-Ampt time inverts its rate, that Manning is Chézy with R**(1/6) / n, that the unit hydrograph conserves volume and superposes, that Theis tends to Cooper-Jacob, that Muskingum routing conserves volume, that Penman-Monteith with zero surface resistance is Penman and that TOPMODEL baseflow falls by e per scaling depth. Glaciology tests check that Glen’s profile with n = 1 is Forbes’s viscous slab, that the shallow-ice flux is the depth integral of Glen’s profile, that Nye’s plastic profile carries the yield stress and Vialov’s profile the accumulated snowfall, that Stefan’s thickness grows at Stefan’s rate, that Haskell’s viscosity inverts the relaxation time, that the Dansgaard-Johnsen age integrates its velocity and tends to Nye’s, and that Schoof’s flux rises as the 4.75 power of the grounding thickness.

Dynamic tests compare the seismic oscillator with its damped analytic solution, climate adjustment with its closed form, dry ascent with conserved potential temperature, inertial motion with analytic rotation/decay, the thermohaline boxes with both steady states of their equilibrium curve, the Rikitake dynamo with its steady states and spontaneous reversals, the eruption column with the pure-plume similarity solution and the Morton-Taylor-Turner height, Green-Ampt infiltration under rain with the Mein-Larson ponding time and closed form, the flowline glacier with a steady flux equal to the mass balance accumulated upstream, and periodic diffusion with a discrete Fourier eigenmode and conserved mean. Plot labels, result immutability, summaries and GIF export are exercised.

Coverage includes every production module, including private validation and plotting utilities. Only test modules are omitted. The test command enforces 100% production statement coverage with no production exclusion pragmas. Branch coverage is not currently configured. Statement execution is neither a proof of scientific correctness nor evidence that a model applies to a real site.

Reproduce from the source checkout:

python -m pip install -e '.[dev]'
MPLBACKEND=Agg python -m pytest --cov=earthsciencekit --cov-report=term-missing
python -m ruff check .
python -m ruff format --check .
MPLBACKEND=Agg python -m sphinx -E -W --keep-going -b html docs/source docs/_build/html
python -m sphinx -W -b doctest docs/source docs/_build/doctest
python -m build

The release’s exact test counts, installed-wheel checks and link checks are recorded in VERIFICATION.md. Examples execute during the HTML build and abort on an error. Scientific teaching references are listed in Scientific teaching references.

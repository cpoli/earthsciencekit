earthsciencekit.hydrology.aerodynamic_resistance(wind_speed, measurement_height, displacement_height, momentum_roughness, vapor_roughness)[source]#

Aerodynamic resistance to vapour transfer above a canopy.

Turbulence in the logarithmic wind profile carries vapour away from the canopy, faster in stronger wind over rougher surfaces.

Parameters:

  • wind_speed (float or array_like) – m/s, at the measurement height; constraint: positive.

  • measurement_height (float or array_like) – m; constraint: positive.

  • displacement_height (float or array_like) – m, zero-plane displacement (about two thirds of the canopy height); constraint: nonnegative.

  • momentum_roughness (float or array_like) – m; constraint: positive.

  • vapor_roughness (float or array_like) – m, often a tenth of the momentum roughness; constraint: positive.

Returns:

Model value [s/m]. Scalar input returns a NumPy scalar; arrays broadcast.

Return type:

numpy scalar or ndarray

Notes

Neutral stability; the measurement height above the displacement height must exceed both roughness lengths. For FAO grass (0.12 m) at 2 m it is about 208 / wind_speed. Equation: np.log((measurement_height - displacement_height) / momentum_roughness) * np.log((measurement_height - displacement_height) / vapor_roughness) / (VON_KARMAN**2 * wind_speed). Invalid, empty, nonreal or nonfinite inputs raise ValueError. NumPy broadcasting applies; singular and overflowing outputs raise ValueError.

earthsciencekit.hydrology.cooper_jacob_drawdown(pumping_rate, transmissivity, storativity, radius, time)[source]#

Cooper and Jacob’s late-time approximation to the Theis drawdown.

Late in a pumping test the drawdown grows linearly with the logarithm of time, so a straight line on a semilog plot gives the aquifer properties.

Parameters:

  • pumping_rate (float or array_like) – m^3/s; constraint: real.

  • transmissivity (float or array_like) – m^2/s; constraint: positive.

  • storativity (float or array_like) – 1; constraint: positive.

  • radius (float or array_like) – m; constraint: positive.

  • time (float or array_like) – s; constraint: positive.

Returns:

Model value [m]. Scalar input returns a NumPy scalar; arrays broadcast.

Return type:

numpy scalar or ndarray

Notes

Cooper and Jacob (1946): the first two terms of the Theis series, within 1% for u < 0.01 and meaningless (it goes negative) for u above about 0.56. Equation: pumping_rate / (4 * np.pi * transmissivity) * (-np.euler_gamma - np.log(radius**2 * storativity / (4 * transmissivity * time))). Invalid, empty, nonreal or nonfinite inputs raise ValueError. NumPy broadcasting applies; singular and overflowing outputs raise ValueError.

Atmosphere examples
===================

One executed example per breakthrough in :doc:`/history/atmosphere_breakthroughs`,
in chronological order, each with an asserted check of the result it shows.
Download the Python source or notebook from each page. :doc:`/guides/atmosphere` records units and
limits; all examples use deterministic synthetic inputs and require no downloads.

    <div class="sphx-glr-thumbcontainer" tooltip="George Hadley explained the trade winds. Air heated at the equator rises and flows poleward aloft, and air returns towards the equator near the ground. The ground there moves eastward faster than at the latitude the air came from, so the returning air lags behind and blows from the east. Today the deflection is written with the Coriolis parameter f = 2 Omega sin(phi). A ring of air that leaves the equator at rest and keeps its angular momentum gains the westerly wind Omega a sin^2(phi) / cos(phi), whose growth with latitude is f + u tan(phi) / a.">

Combined with hydrostatic balance, a rising dry parcel cools at the dry adiabatic lapse rate g / c_p, close to 10 K per kilometre. The wind is geostrophic, blowing along the isobars rather than down the pressure gradient, counter-clockwise round a northern low. The geostrophic components are v = (dp/dx) / (rho f) and u = -(dp/dy) / (rho f). The check is that, at every point, the wind is perpendicular to the gradient and the low lies to its left.">

The equation of state is the diagnostic one that ties pressure, density and temperature together. Moist air obeys it with the virtual temperature in place of temperature, so it is lighter than dry air at the same pressure and temperature. The check is that the result equals the sum of Dalton&#x27;s partial densities.">

Turbulence must lift heavy air against buoyancy, at a rate set by N^2, using energy drawn from the shear. Their ratio, the Richardson number, decides: Miles and Howard later proved that a stratified shear flow can become unstable only where it falls below 1/4. Over a night-time boundary layer, the critical value marks the height where turbulence dies out.">

    <div class="sphx-glr-thumbcontainer" tooltip="In Weather Prediction by Numerical Process, Richardson computed by hand a six-hour forecast for two points over Germany, from the observations of 20 May 1910. He predicted a surface pressure rise of 145 hPa, about a hundred times too large. The pressure tendency is the column-mean divergence times the surface pressure, and the divergence at any one level is much larger than the column mean, because convergence below nearly cancels divergence above. A column-mean error of 7e-6 per second, the size of the divergence at a single level, is enough to reproduce his result.">

Because the Coriolis parameter grows northward at the rate beta, a vorticity wave propagates westward relative to the wind at beta / k^2. Long waves therefore stand still or retrogress, and the stationary wavelength is 2 pi sqrt(U / beta). These waves are large scale, with Rossby numbers well below one.">
